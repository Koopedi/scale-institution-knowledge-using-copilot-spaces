# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality assurance strategies to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and production standards. They collaborate with product and engineering teams to validate solutions before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and acceptance criteria for features
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Report quality metrics and test coverage
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigation plans
- Partner with developers on testability and Definition of Done

### Goals
- Ensure consistent quality and reliability across releases
- Maximize test coverage and catch defects early
- Enable fast feedback loops for the development team

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews and acceptance criteria discussions with Product Managers and Developers
- Quality status reports to Project Managers and stakeholders
- Test metrics and coverage reports in sprint reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with **Developers** to review acceptance criteria, design test cases, and ensure testability
- Reports quality status to **Project Managers** for release readiness decisions
- Validates features against **Product Manager** requirements and success metrics

---

## Tech Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads provide technical direction, design guidance, and risk mitigation. They own technical decisions, mentor developers, and ensure architectural alignment across projects.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical design and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose solutions
- Mentor developers and support capability building
- Ensure consistency with platform standards
- Participate in code review for complex changes

### Goals
- Enable scalable, maintainable architecture
- Reduce technical debt and mitigate technical risks
- Build team technical competency and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Mentoring and code review feedback
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation planning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Guides **Developers** on technical approach, best practices, and design patterns
- Advises **Project Managers** on technical feasibility, effort estimates, and timeline impacts
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on technical trade-offs and feature feasibility
- Partners with **QA/Testing Lead** on testability strategy and test design

---

## Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers own team capacity, career development, and team health. They balance resource allocation across projects and support their team members' growth.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity and resource allocation across projects
- Support career development and skill growth
- Conduct 1-on-1s and provide feedback
- Identify and mitigate team blockers and burnout risks
- Represent team interests in leadership discussions

### Goals
- Maintain high team morale and engagement
- Develop strong technical talent and leaders
- Optimize team productivity and effectiveness

### Typical Communication
- 1-on-1 meetings with direct reports
- Capacity planning and resource allocation discussions
- Career development and feedback sessions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with **Project Managers** on capacity planning, resource allocation, and project staffing
- Supports **Developers** with mentoring, skill development, and career growth opportunities
- Partners with **Product Managers** on prioritization trade-offs and team capacity constraints
- Collaborates with **Tech Lead** on technical talent development and team capability building

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team blockers, and improve team processes. They enable self-organizing teams to deliver effectively and continuously improve.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, retrospectives, and demos
- Identify and help remove team blockers and impediments
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track and report on team metrics and sprint progress
- Help stakeholders understand agile processes and team velocity

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement culture
- Ensure smooth execution of agile ceremonies and practices

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and team meetings
- Impediment escalation and blocker resolution
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Facilitates **Developers** participation in ceremonies and removes blockers
- Works with **Project Managers** on schedule management and dependency coordination
- Supports **Product Managers** with backlog refinement and prioritization processes
- Coaches all team members on agile best practices and team collaboration

---

## Stakeholders & Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors define business priorities, provide approvals, and allocate resources. They represent customer and business interests, set strategic direction, and ensure project alignment with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and strategic objectives
- Provide project sponsorship and executive support
- Approve key decisions and resource allocation
- Communicate project value and progress to the broader organization
- Participate in go/no-go decision gates

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value and ROI
- Maintain strategic alignment across the organization
- Remove organizational barriers and provide executive support

### Typical Communication
- Executive steering committee meetings
- Milestone reviews and status updates
- Decision gate approvals and feedback sessions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provide business context and strategic direction to **Product Managers**
- Approve resource allocation and staffing with **Project Managers**
- Receive escalations from **Project Managers** on risks and decisions
- Approve release and go-live decisions with input from all delivery roles

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure products and processes meet security and compliance requirements. They identify risks, guide secure design practices, and support incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs and code for security risks and vulnerabilities
- Define security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Support threat modeling and risk mitigation
- Coordinate security scanning and vulnerability remediation
- Participate in incident response for security events

### Goals
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and compliance risks
- Embed security practices into the development lifecycle
- Enable rapid, secure incident response

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement and design review discussions
- Threat assessment and mitigation planning
- Security scanning results and remediation tracking

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with **Developers** on secure coding practices and vulnerability remediation
- Advises **Project Managers** on security-related risks, dependencies, and timeline impacts
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on feature security requirements and user data protection
- Works with **Tech Lead** on architectural security decisions and secure design patterns
- Escalates critical vulnerabilities to **Stakeholders** and triggers incident response

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Platform Engineers manage infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, deployments, and observability. They enable reliable, scalable delivery and provide foundational services for the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage cloud infrastructure and environments (dev, staging, production)
- Implement monitoring, logging, and observability solutions
- Support incident response and troubleshooting
- Optimize performance, reliability, and cost of platform services

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable deployments with minimal risk
- Maximize uptime and system observability
- Reduce operational overhead and deployment time

### Typical Communication
- Infrastructure architecture and deployment pipeline reviews
- Incident response and post-mortem discussions
- Platform capability and performance reporting

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with **Developers** on CI/CD pipeline optimization and deployment support
- Partners with **Project Managers** on deployment scheduling and release coordination
- Collaborates with **Tech Lead** on infrastructure architecture and platform decisions
- Supports **QA/Testing Lead** with staging environments and test data management
- Participates in incident response with **Security Lead** and other team members
